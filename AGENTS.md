# Project agent instructions

## Commits

- Remind me to commit whenever we finish a feature or a small, meaningful piece of progress.
- Keep commits focused and regular throughout the project. Do not let completed work accumulate across multiple sessions without a reminder.
- Before ending a work session with uncommitted progress, explicitly remind me what is ready to commit and suggest a short commit message.
- The assignment requires at least one commit per working week; remind me of this requirement when relevant.
- Do not create commits unless I ask you to or have already authorized you to do so.
